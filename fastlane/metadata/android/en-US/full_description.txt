Infinite feeds are built never to run out. END gives them a bottom.

You pick how much feed exists, for example three screens. When you have used it
up, the page stops: the END wall covers the feed with a countdown to your next
one. Tap it and you are out. When the countdown ends, the feed is yours again.

One rule, nothing else. END does not lock your phone, does not nag, does not
score you and does not report on you.

WHAT YOU CAN SET
- How much feed counts as enough, from one screen upward.
- How long the pause lasts.
- Which apps it applies to.
- Which sites it applies to when you open them in a browser.

Every feed is counted separately, so reaching the end of one leaves the others
alone.

PRIVACY
No account. No analytics. No advertising. No third party code. END has no
Internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere, and nothing about you
leaves your phone.

END is free software under the GPLv3. The full source is on GitHub, so anyone
can check exactly what it does and build it themselves.

END uses the Android accessibility service, because that is the only way an app
can tell that a feed is scrolling. It reads that a feed moved and which app is
in front. It never reads page text, messages, images or passwords, and it never
takes over your touches.
