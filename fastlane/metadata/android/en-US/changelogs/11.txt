First public release.

One rule: after the amount of feed you choose, the page stops.

- Works in apps and, per site, in browsers.
- Every feed counted separately.
- No account, no analytics, no Internet permission.
- Setup: opens END's own page in Accessibility settings, with a guide for when Android lands on the list instead.
- Setup: a Done button, and a live status card that shows whether END is counting.
- Fix: counting could stay switched off on devices where the touch sensor never reports.
- Fix: a keyboard or system dialog could be mistaken for the foreground app, which stopped counting in browsers.
