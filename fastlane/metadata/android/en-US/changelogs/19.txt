- Counting is rebuilt on what feeds were measured to report. YouTube Home counts real screens, one Short is one screen, and sites in browsers are no longer counted double.
- The end sits on the exact spot where your limit fell, at the bottom of the screen.
- The end has a way out: in YouTube, one tap on the marker takes you to Subscriptions, which has no end.
- A feed pushed past its end comes straight back, in YouTube, in Shorts and in the browser. Scrolling back stays the app's own.
- The end no longer flickers off when another app shows a bubble or a toolbar.
- "Reset all feeds now" always resets.
